More than a number

A boy from Rosario.
A home in every heart.

He gave Argentina the one thing that had always felt just out of reach: a generation-defining triumph, shared by an entire country.

There were near misses, a goodbye that did not last, and years of carrying the weight of expectation. Then came the Maracanã, the night in Lusail, and one last Copa. Not a perfect story. A human one. That is what made it ours.
